**Core identity**: Hunan First Normal School (known as 'Yishi'), whose predecessor was Chengnan Academy of the Southern Song; Mao Zedong studied and taught here for eight years.

**Key facts**

- The site is a national key cultural relic protection unit; the Class Eight classroom, the well, the Junzi Pavilion, the assembly hall and the reading room are all on campus.
- The school motto 'seeking truth from facts' deeply shaped the young Mao's way of thinking.
