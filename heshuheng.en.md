**Core identity**: Student of the teacher-training course at Hunan First Normal School (entered in 1913 at the age of 37, the oldest in his cohort), the oldest member of the New People's Study Society and a delegate to the First National Congress of the CPC.

**Key facts**

- In 1913 he was admitted to the teacher-training course of the Hunan Provincial Fourth Normal School; when the Fourth Normal School merged into Hunan First Normal School the following year, he met Mao Zedong and Cai Hesen and became their friend across the generation gap.
- In 1918 he, Mao Zedong and Cai Hesen jointly initiated the New People's Study Society, of which he was the oldest member.
- In the winter of 1920 he and Mao Zedong jointly founded the early Communist organisation in Hunan, and in 1921 the two travelled to Shanghai to attend the CPC's First National Congress.
- Mao Zedong said of him: 'Uncle He can be entrusted with the big picture.'
